# Safety Guidelines

Electrical muscle stimulation (EMS) affects the human body. Use bioGen-EMS 2.0 only with appropriate training, ethics approval, and informed consent.

## Before every session

1. Obtain institutional ethics approval for human-participant research.
2. Explain the procedure and obtain informed consent.
3. Inspect electrodes and cables for damage.
4. Set the external EMS unit to the **lowest effective intensity**.
5. Run `r` (reset all channels) before attaching electrodes.
6. Calibrate using Auto-Paizo or serial EMG readout before gameplay.

## Electrode placement

- Follow manufacturer guidance for the EMS unit and electrodes.
- Do **not** place stimulation electrodes on the head, neck, chest, or broken skin.
- Avoid stimulation across the heart.
- Use only intended muscle groups for your study protocol.

## During use

- Participants must be able to stop stimulation immediately (researcher disconnects or sends `r`).
- Monitor for pain, burns, skin irritation, or unexpected muscle reactions.
- Keep sessions within approved duration and intensity limits.
- Never leave stimulation unattended.

## Contraindications (consult a qualified clinician)

Do not use EMS on participants with pacemakers, pregnancy, epilepsy, or other conditions where electrical stimulation is contraindicated unless explicitly cleared by medical oversight.

## Hardware

- Verify relay wiring before connecting the EMS unit.
- Use battery or isolated supplies as designed; avoid mains-connected DIY stim circuits.
- This kit **switches** an off-the-shelf EMS device; it does not replace certified medical device safety review.

## Research documentation

Record calibration values, channel mappings, and any adverse events per your ethics protocol.
